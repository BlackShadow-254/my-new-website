This was a phishing-awareness simulation

No password, payment information, or other sensitive data was collected. The purpose of this exercise is to help you recognize suspicious messages.

What happened?

The email used an invoice or payment scenario to test whether the request would be verified through an approved process before action was taken.

Unexpected payment request

Treat unfamiliar invoices or urgent payment reminders as suspicious until confirmed.

Link in the email

Check the destination before clicking and use the official finance or procurement portal where possible.

Vendor details

Compare the vendor, purchase order, amount, and account details with trusted internal records.

Urgency

Pressure to pay quickly is not a reason to bypass normal approval and verification controls.

Important: Never approve a payment or change bank details based only on an email. Confirm the request using a trusted phone number, official portal, or established internal workflow.
What to do next time
  1. Pause before opening links or attachments.
  2. Check the sender address and link destination.
  3. Verify invoices independently with Finance or Procurement.
  4. Report suspicious messages using the approved reporting method.
